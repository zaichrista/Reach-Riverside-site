It's always more than good food

Good food, made for sharing and enjoying. Our menu brings together generous flavours, quality ingredients and much-loved classics, alongside fresh seasonal dishes.

From relaxed lunches and leisurely afternoons to dinner by the river, there's something for every occasion.

Whether you're joining us for a quick bite, sharing plates with friends or settling in for the evening, expect delicious food, generous portions and dishes you'll want to come back for.
