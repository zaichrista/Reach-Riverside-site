The restaurant

What is The Reach Riverside?

The Reach Riverside is a family-run Mediterranean restaurant, bar and lounge by the river in Fulham Reach, West London. Every dish is made with love and meant to be shared, and we serve brunch, lunch, coffee, dinner and cocktails every day.

Where is The Reach Riverside?

We are at Goldhurst House, Parr's Way, London W6 9AN, in Fulham Reach. We are two minutes' walk from Riverside Studios and five minutes' walk from the Eventim Apollo. View on Google Maps.

Is The Reach Riverside family-run?

Yes. We are a family-run restaurant, and the idea behind everything on our menu is simple: food made with love, to be shared.

What type of food do you serve?

Mediterranean food, with dishes from Spain, Greece and Lebanon, alongside British favourites with a twist. Our highlight is our small plates, in the spirit of tapas and mezze, made to be shared across the table. Our food is sourced in Britain.

Do you serve tapas and mezze?

Our small plates are our version of tapas and mezze: dishes for sharing, such as padrón peppers, gambas al ajillo, patatas bravas, hummus, tzatziki, smoked aubergine and grilled halloumi. Order a few for the table and keep adding.

Do you serve British food as well?

Yes. Our Riverside Classics are British favourites with a twist, including fish and chips, The Reach cheeseburger and chicken schnitzel, and we serve a Sunday roast every Sunday. See our menus.

Is The Reach Riverside a restaurant, a bar or a lounge?

All three. It is a restaurant for brunch, lunch and dinner, a bar with cocktails created by our in-house mixologist, and a lounge where you are welcome to stay a little longer.

Where does your food come from?

Our food is locally sourced, from within Britain.

Opening hours and booking

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 11am until midnight.

What time does the kitchen close?

Our kitchen closes at 10pm. We serve drinks until midnight.

Do you serve drinks until midnight?

Yes. We serve drinks until midnight, so you can stay on for a cocktail or a glass of wine after your meal.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes, we are open every day, including Sundays, when we serve our Sunday roast from 12 midday until 4pm.

How do I book a table?

You can book online or call us on 020 8167 7999.

Can I book a table for a large group?

Yes. Send us the date, the numbers and the occasion through our group bookings and events page or email info@thereachriverside.com, and our team will come back to you.

Do you have a lunch offer?

What is the express lunch?

Do you have a happy hour?

Brunch in Fulham Reach

Do you serve brunch in Fulham Reach?

Yes. We serve brunch by the river in Fulham Reach, with a Mediterranean twist. Slow starts are very welcome, and dogs and children are too.

What is on your brunch menu?

Eggs Benedict, Eggs Royale, Chorizo Hash, Avocado on Toast, French Toast and our Mediterranean Brunch of fried eggs, grilled halloumi, smashed avocado, sautéed garlic mushrooms, feta, patatas and toasted sourdough. See the brunch menu.

Is there a vegetarian brunch?

Yes. Avocado on Toast, French Toast and our Mediterranean Brunch are all marked vegetarian.

Is brunch dog friendly?

Yes. Dogs are welcome at brunch, inside and out.

Where can I get brunch and a cocktail in Fulham?

At The Reach Riverside, by the river in Fulham Reach. Our brunch and a cocktail offer is £16.

Lunch and Sunday roast

Do you serve lunch?

Yes, every day. Choose from small plates to share, salads, our Riverside Classics and dishes from the grill, or come Tuesday to Friday for our £25 two-course express lunch between 12 midday and 3pm.

Do you serve Sunday roast?

Yes. We serve Sunday roast every Sunday from 12 midday until 4pm: roast beef, lamb, chicken or a cauliflower steak, all served with Yorkshire pudding, roast potatoes, seasonal vegetables and house gravy. You can add cauliflower cheese.

Is there a vegetarian Sunday roast?

Yes. Our vegetarian roast is a cauliflower steak, served with Yorkshire pudding, roast potatoes, seasonal vegetables and house gravy.

Where can I find a Sunday roast in Fulham?

At The Reach Riverside, by the river in Fulham Reach, every Sunday from 12 midday until 4pm. See what is on.

Dinner, cocktails and drinks

Do you serve dinner?

Yes, every evening. Our kitchen closes at 10pm, and we serve drinks until midnight. Dinner can be small plates to share, a Riverside Classic or something from the grill.

Do you have a cocktail menu?

Yes. Our in-house mixologist has created a cocktail menu especially for The Reach Riverside, with signature cocktails such as Sunday Morning Linen, Second Date, Cloud Kyu and The Smashed One, alongside classic cocktails.

Is there a cocktail bar in Fulham Reach?

Yes. The Reach Riverside is a restaurant, bar and lounge by the river in Fulham Reach, serving cocktails until midnight and happy hour from 4pm to 7pm.

What wine do you serve?

We have a curated international wine list, with sparkling, white, rosé and red wines, dessert wine and 0% alcohol wines.

Do you have non-alcoholic drinks?

Yes. We serve mocktails, including a No-ABV Margarita, a Lavender Lemonade Fizz and a Virgin Cosmopolitan, as well as 0% alcohol wines.

Do you serve beer and coffee?

Yes. We have a selection of beers, and we serve coffee for slow starts, long lunches and afternoons.

Can I come just for a drink?

Yes. We are a bar and lounge as well as a restaurant, and we serve drinks until midnight.

Gluten free, vegetarian and vegan

Do you have gluten free options?

Yes. We have a gluten free option for all of our bread, and many dishes across the menu are marked (GF). Please tell your server about any allergies or intolerances before ordering.

Is your bread gluten free?

We have a gluten free option for all of our bread, so you can still enjoy the sharing plates.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Vegetarian dishes are marked (V) on our menus, and include hummus, smoked aubergine, tzatziki, chilli cauliflower, grilled halloumi, several salads and our Moorish couscous.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. Dishes marked (VG) include padrón peppers, sweet potato fries, sautéed garlic green beans and our house salad, and our team can talk you through the rest.

What if I have an allergy or intolerance?

Please inform your server of any allergies or intolerances before placing your order. Our menus mark dishes as (V) vegetarian, (VG) vegan, (GF) gluten free and (N) contains nuts.

Groups, birthdays and events

Do you take group bookings?

Yes. Tell us the date, the numbers and the occasion through our group dining and events page or email info@thereachriverside.com, and our team will come back to you. For anything urgent, call 020 8167 7999.

Do you have a private dining room?

We do not have a separate private room. We host groups at long tables in the restaurant, with generous plates to share and a team who look after the details.

Can I book a birthday dinner in Fulham?

Yes. We love hosting birthdays. Long tables, plates made for sharing and cocktails from our in-house mixologist make it easy to celebrate. Send us the date and numbers and we will come back to you.

Can I book a business or company dinner?

Yes. We host business dinners, company dinners and corporate meetings. Tell us the date, the numbers and what you need, and our team will come back to you.

Do you host Mother's Day, Valentine's and other holidays?

Yes. We host all the holidays, including Christmas, Mother's Day and Valentine's. Get in touch with the date and the numbers and we will take it from there.

Can you arrange entertainment for my event?

Yes. For celebrations we can arrange a DJ, saxophonist, violinist, ballerinas, a singer and more.

Can I add canapés or a drinks package?

Yes. For Christmas celebrations, canapés are available from £15 per person, and drinks options include Prosecco on arrival, a half bottle of house wine, drink tokens and a two-hour drinks package.

Christmas at The Reach

Do you host Christmas parties in Fulham?

Yes. We host Christmas lunch and dinner for work parties, family gatherings and friends, by the river in Fulham Reach. See Christmas at The Reach.

When is your Christmas menu available?

Our Christmas menu and packages are available from 9 November to 28 December 2026, for lunch and dinner, Monday to Sunday. Early booking is recommended for peak dates.

How much are your Christmas packages?

Festive Lunch (two courses) is £45 per person, Festive Lunch or Dinner (three courses) is £55, the Christmas Celebration with Prosecco on arrival is £65, and The Reach Party, with Prosecco and a half bottle of house wine each, is £79. All prices are per person.

Do you have a Christmas option for larger parties?

Yes. Our Festive Sharing Feast is £49 per person, a relaxed Mediterranean-inspired feast for larger parties.

How do I book a Christmas party?

Email info@thereachriverside.com with the date, the numbers and the occasion, and our team will check availability and tailor your event. You can also book online.
